HOW TO PLAY
  1. One player taps NEW GAME and shares the room code.
  2. On the other device, enter the code and tap JOIN GAME.
  3. Place your 5 ships: tap a ship, then tap the grid. ROTATE turns it. Tap a placed ship to move it.
  4. Take turns firing at enemy waters. RED = HIT, GREEN = MISS.
  5. Sink the whole enemy fleet to win the round.
  6. Keep both screens awake. If a device sleeps, the link can drop.
