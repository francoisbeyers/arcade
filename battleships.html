HOW TO PLAY
  1. Hide your ships: tap a ship, then tap the sea. TURN spins it. Or tap MIX UP to place them all.
  2. Take turns firing at the enemy sea.
  3. RED = HIT. GREEN = MISS.
  4. Sink every enemy ship to win!
  5. QUICK uses a smaller sea with 4 ships for shorter games. BIG is the classic 10 by 10 with 5 ships.
  6. 2 PLAYERS on one device: pass it over when it says so, and no peeking!
