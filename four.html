FOUR
IN A ROW

HOW TO PLAY
  1. Take turns dropping a counter into a column. Tap anywhere in the column.
  2. Get 4 of your colour in a line to win: across, up and down, or slanted.
  3. Block your friend when they have 3 in a line!
  4. If the board fills up with no winner, it's a draw.
